Privacy Policy

DJ Tracker — Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how DJ Tracker collects, uses, and protects the information you provide when using our mobile application.

By using DJ Tracker, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following personal information when you register or use the app:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Google or Apple account information (when signing in with Google or Apple)

2. Session and Usage Information

To provide the app's core features, we collect:

  • Song titles and artist names identified during your DJ sessions (via Apple's ShazamKit) — we do not record or store audio itself, only the identified song information
  • Session start/end times and duration
  • Your remaining and used session credit balance

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Track and display your DJ session history
  • Generate exportable session reports (Excel)
  • Process session-pack purchases and maintain your session balance
  • Send account-related communications (e.g. email verification) via Firebase

4. Email Communications

We send transactional emails such as account verification and password reset emails. These are handled through Firebase (Firebase Privacy Policy). We do not send marketing emails.

5. Third-Party Services

DJ Tracker uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication, database, storage: Privacy Policy
  • Google Sign-In — social login: Privacy Policy
  • Apple Sign-In — social login, and ShazamKit — song identification: Apple Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store / Google Play Billing — payment processing for session-pack purchases. We never see or store your card details

6. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Firebase Cloud Firestore. We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and data at any time from within the app.

8. Children's Privacy

DJ Tracker is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

9. Advertising

DJ Tracker does not display any advertisements.

10. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

12. Account Deletion

You can delete your account directly from the app:

  • Go to the Settings screen
  • Scroll down and tap "Delete Account"
  • Confirm deletion

When you delete your account:

  • ✅ Your profile data is permanently deleted
  • ✅ Your authentication account is removed from Firebase
  • ✅ Your remaining session credits and purchase history are deleted
