Privacy Policy — DJ Tracker

Last updated: August 2026

DJ Tracker ("the app") is developed by [YOUR CLIENT'S NAME/COMPANY]. This policy explains what information we collect, how it's used, and how it's protected.

Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address and name — used to create and manage your account (via Firebase Authentication, and optionally Google or Apple Sign-In)
  • Session data — the songs detected during your DJ sessions, session start/end times, and durations, so you can view your history and export reports
  • Purchase records — which session packs you've purchased, to maintain your account balance. Payment itself is handled entirely by Apple and Google — we never see or store your card details

How We Use Song Detection

DJ Tracker uses Apple's ShazamKit to identify songs playing during your sessions. Audio is processed only while a session is actively running and only to identify tracks — we do not record or store audio itself, only the resulting song title and artist information.

Where Your Data Is Stored

Account and session data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore and Authentication), a widely used cloud platform with industry-standard security practices. Some session history is also stored locally on your device.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. Your data is shared only with:

  • Apple and Google, solely to process purchases and verify subscriptions/refunds
  • Firebase (Google Cloud), our cloud infrastructure provider

Your Choices

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings within the app. This permanently removes your account, remaining session credits, and purchase history.

Children's Privacy

DJ Tracker is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
