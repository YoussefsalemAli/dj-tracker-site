Common Questions

How does song detection work?
DJ Tracker automatically listens and identifies songs during a live session using Apple's ShazamKit — no manual entry needed.

What happens if I lose signal or my phone locks?
Detection runs while the app is open and active. If you switch apps or the screen locks, detection pauses and resumes automatically when you return.

How do sessions work?
Each session covers one gig, automatically ending after 12 hours if you forget to stop it manually.

How do I get more sessions?
Open the app, tap the sessions counter on the Dashboard, and choose a session pack to purchase.

How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings inside the app and choose Delete Account. This is permanent.
